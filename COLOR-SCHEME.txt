Last updated: 2026-10-28

================================================================================
1. DIRECTION
================================================================================

Near-black editorial dark theme with a single electric cyan / sky accent. No light mode.
High-contrast white typography, quiet charcoal surfaces, and cyan reserved for
precision, system diagnostics, and interactive feedback.

Design intent:
- Premium, corporate, engineering-led appearance inspired by elite developer tooling (modern developer tooling).
- High contrast and generous negative space.
- One accent family only (electric cyan/sky).
- Elevation communicated through hairline borders, not heavy shadows.
- Motion and hover states use a soft cyan "aura" glow representing code compilation and system readiness.

3. SURFACES & ELEVATION
================================================================================

Page background:
  radial-gradient(1100px 700px at 50% 0%, #0f172a 0%, #050505 55%), #050505
  A subtle cool slate halo at the top, fading into near-black.

Header:
  Background: #050505 at 80% opacity
  Effect: backdrop-blur-xl
  Border-bottom: white at 10% opacity (~#1d1d1d)

Cards (experience, education, certifications, contact):
  Resting surface: white at 2% over base  (~#0a0a0a)
  Hover surface:   white at 4% over base  (~#0f0f0f)
  Border:          white at 10%           (~#1d1d1d)

Inputs:
  Background: black at 40% opacity        (~#060606)
  Border:     white at 10%                (~#1d1d1d)
  Focus:      cyan-500 border + 40% ring

Borders and dividers:
  Standard border: white 10%  (~#1d1d1d)
  Subtle divider:  white 5%   (~#0f0f0f)
  Hover border:    cyan-500 at 50%
  Accent border:   cyan-500 at 30-50%

State surfaces:
  Success: emerald-950/30 background, emerald-900/60 border, emerald-300 text
  Error:   red-950/30 background,     red-900/60 border,     red-300 text

Typography behavior:
  - Headings use tracking-tight.
  - Body copy uses leading-relaxed.
  - Eyebrows use uppercase letter-spacing (~0.18em).
  - Font stack is currently the system sans stack (no custom typeface loaded).
  - Terminal/code areas use a monospace stack.

================================================================================
7. NOTES / DESIGN AUTHENTICITY
================================================================================

- The cyan palette shifts the portfolio tone from aggressive/passionate (red) to precision engineering, systems reliability, and software quality assurance (teal/cyan enterprise aesthetic used by Microsoft, GitHub, and Linear).
- Tailwind v4 cyan tokens (#0891b2, #06b6d4, #38bdf8) replace red tokens cleanly across all button fills, glowing aura shadows, and borders.
- There is no light theme, maintaining high-end developer immersion.
- System sans stack is retained for lightning-fast edge rendering.
